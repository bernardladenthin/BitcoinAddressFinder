Notes. One message carries one base, not one key: the device expands it into 2batchSizeInBits candidates, so a few dozen messages per second are enough to keep a GPU busy. The lookahead exists because the server's queue is unbounded — keep it small, since anything still queued when the process stops was never checked, and only the batches reported back here are known to be done. Results are broadcast to every connected client and contain private keys.
